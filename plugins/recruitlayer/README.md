# recruitlayer

Use Claude to work with live recruiting and sales data from recruitlayer. Sales
and account teams can review leads, find postings that are about to expire, and
compare click delivery. Recruiting teams can draft and submit new job postings,
edit saved jobs, and review applications in their hiring pipelines.

You need a recruitlayer account. Connect it through the OAuth sign-in prompt.
Every result and action follows the signed-in user's current recruitlayer access.

- **Sales briefing** prioritizes accounts, leads, and postings that need attention.
- **Job postings** helps draft, edit, or troubleshoot a job posting.
- **Candidate review** organizes applications and applicant counts for a qualified
  reviewer. Hiring decisions require human oversight and disclosure to affected
  candidates when AI is used in a decision affecting their employment.
